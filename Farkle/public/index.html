Project 1: Juggling with Sockets

A phone-first live experience: two or more devices play together in real time via Socket.IO. No auth, no database—just the game while everyone is connected.

This project: Farkle—multiplayer dice game. First to 10,000 points wins. Deployed on the Hong Kong server; run node server.js before presenting and share the URL (or QR code).

      Game Rules

      • Objective: Be the first to reach 10,000 points.
      • Turns: Only the current player (green outline) can roll. Others wait until their turn.
      • Roll: Single 1s = 100 pts, single 5s = 50 pts. Three-of-a-kind = 200–600 pts (three 1s = 1000). Straight 1–6 = 1500 pts.
      • Farkle: If a roll has no scoring dice, you lose all points for that turn and play passes to the next player.
      • Bank: Save your turn points to your total and pass the dice to the next player.
      • Hot dice: If all six dice score, you may roll all six again and keep adding to your turn.
      • Winning: First player to reach 10,000+ wins. Use New Game to play again.
